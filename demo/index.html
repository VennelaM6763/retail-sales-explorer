Data cleaning and interpretation

Sales are Quantity × UnitPrice in GBP, summed over positive transactions. This is not net revenue or profit: refunds and costs are not included. Product rows may include service or postage codes. Missing customer IDs remain in sales totals but are excluded from the known-customer count. Removing exact duplicates assumes repeated identical rows are accidental; genuine repeated lines could be removed.

Customer counts are unique within the selected market and period, so they must not be added across months. Average order value is positive sales divided by distinct invoices. Observational differences do not establish causation.
